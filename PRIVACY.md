# Tabloid privacy policy

Tabloid is a new tab page extension. It is built to keep your information on your own device.

## What Tabloid stores

Your dashboard layout, widget settings (including feed addresses, links, notes and to-dos), appearance choices, feed cache and read history are stored in your browser's extension storage on your device.

## What leaves your device

- **Nothing goes to the developer.** Tabloid has no servers of its own, and no analytics, tracking or ads.
- **Sync, only if you turn it on.** You can sync your setup through your browser account, Dropbox, a WebDAV server or a GitHub repository. Your setup then goes only to the service you chose, under your own account. Sign-in details for that service stay on your device and are never synced or exported.
- **Content you ask for.** To show your widgets, Tabloid fetches what you added from the sites themselves: RSS feeds and their sites' icons, preview images for your quick links, the pages in a Webpages widget, weather from Open-Meteo for the place you picked, and background photos from Bing, Wikimedia or Unsplash if you chose one. Those sites see an ordinary request from your browser.
- **Search** sends what you type to the search engine you chose, when you search.

## Website access

Tabloid asks for access to websites only when a feature you turn on needs it. Screenshots for quick links are taken only of pages that match one of your links, only in normal (not private) windows, and are kept on your device.

## Your control

You can export your setup, delete widgets, or remove the extension at any time. Removing it deletes everything it stored on your device. Data you synced stays in your own account with that service until you delete it there.
